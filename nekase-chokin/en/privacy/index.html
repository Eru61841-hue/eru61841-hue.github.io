Sleep On It Privacy Policy

Effective November 1, 2026

This policy explains how the app "Sleep On It" (Japanese name: ねかせ貯金; "the App") handles your information.

1. Information the App handles

The App does not require an account and never asks you to enter information that identifies you, such as your name, email address, phone number, or location.

The following data, created as you use the App, is stored on your device:

• Names and prices of the items you enter
• How much you want each item
• When items were added, unlock, and were decided
• Your decisions (Skipped or Worth it)
• App settings (unlock-day reminders)

Prices are numbers you enter for your own records. The App does not connect to bank accounts, credit cards, or any other financial service, and it does not process payments.

2. How stored data is handled

This data is never sent to the developer's servers or to any third party (for information collected by ad providers, see "Advertising"). The developer cannot see it.

Deleting the App deletes this data from your device. You can also erase it in the App under Settings > "Delete all records".

3. Notifications

Unlock-day reminders are local notifications scheduled and shown on your device. The App does not contact any external server to send them. Reminders show the item name. You can turn them off at any time in Settings.

4. Share images

When you export your stats or a decision as an image, the image and its accompanying text are created entirely on your device. They may include dates, prices, decision counts, item names, and how many days you slept on an item. You choose whether and where to share them, and the service you share to handles them under its own terms and policies.

5. Advertising

The App shows ads from Google AdMob (a banner at the bottom of the screen, and a full-screen ad when you close the screen after a decision). To show ads, Google and its partner ad providers may collect information such as device identifiers and usage data. This information is handled under Google's privacy policy, not by the developer of the App.

On iOS, you may be asked whether to allow tracking for personalized ads. If you do not allow it, you will still see ads, but they will not be personalized. You can change this at any time in your device settings.

Item names, prices, and decision records stored by the App are never sent for advertising.

Google's privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

6. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated as needed. Significant changes will be announced in the App or in its App Store description.
