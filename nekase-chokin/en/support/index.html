Sleep On It Support

Want it? Sleep on it first. An app that stops impulse buys.

Add something you want, let it sleep for a few days, and on unlock day decide once more: "Still want it?" Everything you decide not to buy adds up as savings. (Japanese name: ねかせ貯金)

FAQ

Q. Where are my records stored?

A. Only on your iPhone. They are never sent to any server. They are included in your iPhone backup (iCloud or computer), so restoring a backup to a new iPhone brings them along.

Q. What happens if I delete the app?

A. All your records are deleted with it and cannot be recovered.

Q. I don't get unlock-day reminders.

A. Check that reminders are "On" in the app's Settings, and that notifications are allowed in the iPhone Settings app > Notifications > Sleep On It. Items that unlock between 10 PM and 8 AM are announced at 8 AM.

Q. Are the comparisons ("41 lattes" etc.) accurate?

A. They are rough estimates based on typical prices. Actual prices vary by place and store.

Q. How do I delete all my records?

A. In the app, go to Settings > "Delete all records". Deleted records cannot be recovered.
